Privacy Policy for Legend Clan Bot

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Legend Clan Bot (the "Bot") collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

To function properly and provide its AI conversational features, the Bot may collect and store the following information:

  • Discord User IDs & Usernames: To identify who is interacting with the Bot.
  • Message Content: The Bot stores the content of messages sent in explicitly configured public discussion channels (like #lobby or #questions-answers) to maintain context and allow the AI to answer community questions intelligently.
  • Player Profiles: If you voluntarily link your game profile or statistics using the Bot's commands, this data is stored in our database for quick retrieval.

2. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide accurate, context-aware AI responses using large language models (such as Groq).
  • Remember community knowledge and past discussions to avoid repeating answers.
  • Display your game statistics and profile when requested via commands.

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

All collected data is stored locally in secure JSON files on our private Virtual Private Server (VPS). Only authorized developers have access to this server. We take reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access or alteration of the data.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses third-party AI APIs (like Groq) to process text and generate answers. When processing a question, only the relevant chat context is sent to the AI API for processing. The API providers are bound by their own privacy policies and do not use this temporary data to train their base models.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data (such as your player profile or specific messages you sent) from the Bot's memory. To request data deletion, please contact the server administrators in the Legend Clan Discord server, and we will manually remove your entries from our database files.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify the community of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the Discord server.

7. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the administrative team in the Legend Clan Discord server.
