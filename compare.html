📊 Verdict

Legend Mod leads with the most features overall — 60+ macros, animated skins, WASM acceleration, gamepad support, multibox, and the only mod that supports Sigmally, Senpa.io, and has its own private server (Expanding Land). It's also the only mod with Firefox and mobile support, and it's fully open source.

Delta is a solid alternative with good WebGL rendering and team features, but lacks multi-server support, animated skins, and mobile/Firefox compatibility.
