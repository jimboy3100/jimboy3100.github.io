About us

Legend Clan — About us 🔥

This is the official space for Legend Clan LC's social networks and announcements.


Here you will find all the essential information about the clan, including official announcements, internal rules, events, achievements and the historical records of our journey.


📜 History and archives: follow the evolution of Legend Clan LC through our public archives, where we gather articles, guides, timelines and exclusive content.


The links are always available in the matching posts.

⚔️ Rules and ethics: we value respect, loyalty and team spirit.


Fair play is mandatory for all members. Any breach of the rules can lead to warnings or, in more serious cases, expulsion from the clan.


📢 Announcements and events: watch the official announcements so you don't miss events, important updates and the clan's administrative decisions.


💬 Contact and support: for questions, support or administrative matters, contact us on our official Discord or send a message directly to the administrators.
