Who we are

A community built match after match

Legend Clan was born inside Agar.io and grew beyond the game: today we bring players, moderators and content creators together on one server, with a public record of achievements, rules and announcements since 2017.

We value fair play, team spirit and respect between members. Breaking the internal rules can lead to a warning or expulsion: we keep the clan serious without losing the fun.

Everything you need (history, articles, contracts with elite players and the list of moderators) is documented and open to every member.
