Legend Mod | Official Project Facts

Legend Mod is a browser client and extension project for Agar.io-style cell games, with custom skins, themes, configurable controls, multiboxing and replay tools.

Expanding Land is a free multiplayer browser cell game with a dynamic circular arena that expands and contracts, FFA and Teams modes, and a C-based server. Human and server-controlled bot populations vary by arena.
